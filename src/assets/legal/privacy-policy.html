1. About this policy

Fine ur Spine ("the App") is a general fitness and wellness mobile application operated by Mohammed Jamsheer Kaitha Valappil, an individual based in India ("we", "us", and "our"). This Privacy Policy explains the personal information the App may collect or access, how it is used and shared, and how to ask questions or request deletion.

The App provides guided workouts, recurring exercise schedules, wellness content, and optional activity features. It is intended for adults aged 18 and above and is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, physiotherapy, or rehabilitation service.

2. Information we collect

Account and sign-in information

We process your name, email address, account identifier, and sign-in method to create and maintain your account. Authentication is provided through Google Firebase Authentication and supported sign-in methods. If email-and-password login is available, Firebase manages password authentication; the App's own database does not need to store your plaintext password. Third-party sign-in does not give us your Google or Apple password.

Authentication and hosting services may process IP addresses, device details, sign-in events, and related technical logs for account security and service operation.

Workout preferences and activity

We may store introductory workout responses, chosen workout level, plan preferences, your recurring day-by-day schedule, video or session completion status, favorites, and related settings. These records support workout selection and progress tracking across sessions.

Post-workout feedback (not currently available)

The current closed-testing version of the App does not offer a post-workout feedback form, so users cannot submit these responses through this version. If this feature is introduced in a later version, it may allow you to optionally select Energetic, Relaxed, or Tired after a workout. Any responses then submitted may be linked to your account and session and used to operate and improve the general fitness experience, not for medical diagnosis. Authorized administrators may access relevant responses when needed to operate the service.

Optional step counts

If step tracking is offered in your version of the App and you grant the required permission, the App may access daily step totals from supported device activity sources, such as Health Connect on Android or Apple health or motion services on iPhone. The exact source depends on the app version and device. When synchronization is enabled, step totals may be saved with your App account so you can view your activity.

Permission is optional and may be withdrawn through your device settings. Withdrawing permission stops future permitted access but does not itself delete previously saved totals. We do not use step data for advertising, marketing profiles, or unrelated data mining.

Water log and reminders

If available and used, water-intake entries and dates may be saved to show your log. Reminder preferences and relevant notification permissions may also be processed. These are general wellness features, not medical advice.

Subscription and discount information, if enabled

When paid subscriptions are enabled, we may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, entitlement periods, and discount eligibility to control access. Google Play or Apple's App Store processes store payments. We do not collect payment-card numbers or card security codes through the App. During a closed test, selected testers may be given access without a payment. Patient-related discounts are only applicable if offered and activated in the released build.

Video delivery, support, and technical records

When you access videos or other content, media delivery services may receive IP addresses, content request details, and technical device information necessary to serve content. We also process requests sent to our support email. Server logs and error data may be used to troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and maintain the service.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to authenticate users, deliver workouts, maintain schedules, save individual choices and progress, display optional wellness data, manage test access or purchases where applicable, respond to requests, and protect and maintain the App.

We do not sell or rent personal information or share it with advertisers for their own marketing purposes.

4. Service providers and access

We use third-party technology and infrastructure to operate the App. Depending on the build and deployment, these services may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: user authentication and sign-in security.
  • Application hosting and PostgreSQL database services: secure operation of the backend, accounts, settings, and workout records. Hosting may change between pre-release testing and production.
  • Media hosting and delivery services, including Bunny.net where integrated: delivery of videos and documents.
  • Google Play or Apple App Store: purchases and subscription handling, if those features are active.

The operator and authorized support or development personnel may access information only as needed to operate, secure, improve, or support the App. Providers may process data in India or other countries, depending on their infrastructure. We may also disclose information when legally required.

5. Security and storage

We use HTTPS connections, account authentication, access controls for application services, and reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Information and backups may be stored or processed by the infrastructure providers used for the App.

6. Data retention and account deletion

Account and workout records are generally kept while the account is active or as reasonably needed to provide the service. Uninstalling the App does not delete an account or server-side information.

You may request deletion of your Fine ur Spine account and its associated personal information. This normally includes account/profile data, saved workout activity, favorites, and stored optional step or water data, as well as any optional workout feedback collected if that feature is introduced. Some records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, security, or accounting reasons. Information in routine backups may remain temporarily until those backups expire or are overwritten. Payment records held independently by an app store are subject to that store's practices.

How to request deletion

  • From within the App: use the account-deletion option or link provided in the app's account settings.
  • Outside the App: visit Fine ur Spine Account & Data Deletion or email fineurspinefitness@gmail.com with the subject Fine ur Spine Account Deletion Request.

We may verify that you control the account before acting on a request. Do not email your password. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an app-store subscription; cancel any active subscription separately through the relevant store.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or raise a privacy concern, at fineurspinefitness@gmail.com. If post-workout feedback becomes available in a future version, you may choose not to submit it. You can manage device permissions for step tracking and notifications in device settings.

8. Children

Fine ur Spine is intended for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite children under 18 to create accounts. If we learn that an ineligible child has an account, we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear on this webpage, and we will provide further notice or obtain permission where required by law.
